# Consent form for recording and analyzing video

> Version `2026-10-27`. Draft, **not legal advice**. English translation of the [Spanish original](../formato-consentimiento-jugadores.md); if they differ, the Spanish version prevails.

The **coach or club** uses it before recording: it is the evidence of the consent they declare in the app ([video consent](video-consent.md#2-your-declarations)). It is printed or sent as a form, and the club keeps it, not PP-Coach-Tracker.

- One form per adult player, or per minor signed by their father, mother or guardian.
- It is valid for the season or the period it states; there is no need for one per match.
- If someone does not sign or withdraws their consent, they are not recorded, or the videos where they appear are deleted (see "If someone does not consent").

### What we will do

1. **Record** the team's matches and practices with a phone fixed on a tripod. The video has **no audio**.
2. **Analyze it with PP-Coach-Tracker**, an app that uses artificial intelligence (including Meta models) to track the players and the ball and compute tactical statistics: positions, heat maps, distances, pressing and recommendations for the team.
3. **Use the results only within the team**, to train and improve the game.

### What we will NOT do

- Publish the videos on social media, the internet or the press without a separate permission.
- Use facial recognition or identify anyone by their features. Each player appears in the system as a number; the name, if added, is only seen by the coach.
- Use the videos to train artificial intelligence models, or sell them or share them with third parties beyond the providers needed for the analysis.
- Use the statistics for medical, hiring or disciplinary decisions without human review.

The video is deleted from the PP-Coach-Tracker server after the analysis (at most `[30]` days). The statistics are kept as long as the team uses them. More details in the privacy notice: `https://ppcoachtracker.pepemxl.com/legal/en/privacy.html`.

### Your rights

You can withdraw this consent at any time by telling the person responsible for recording. From then on you will not be recorded, and the videos and statistics in PP-Coach-Tracker where you appear will be deleted. Withdrawing it does not affect your place on the team.

You can also ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to the person responsible for recording or to `[PRIVACY EMAIL]`.

## If someone does not consent

- **Do not record** the sessions they take part in, or record only the drills where they do not appear.
- If they appear by accident (for example, an opponent or a spectator), they are not tagged and the clip is not used outside the internal analysis.
- If a player withdraws their consent, delete in the app the clips where they appear (that deletes the video and the analytics in PP-Coach-Tracker).

## Opponents, referees and spectators

The form covers your own team. For matches against other teams:

- Tell the opposing team and the referees before the match that it will be recorded for analysis, ideally through the league.
- If the league has rules about recording, follow them; in youth leagues, ask the league to include recording in its rules or in the registration.
- Do not tag opposing players by name; in PP-Coach-Tracker they stay only as a number.
