This notice complies with Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP) and its regulations.

## 2. Who it applies to

- **Users:** coaches, clubs or people who create an account in the pp-coach-tracker app.
- **People in the videos:** players, referees and other people who appear in the videos users upload. For them, the user who records is the controller and the one who collects their consent; PP-Coach-Tracker processes the videos on the user's behalf and following their instructions.

## 3. What data we process

| Category | Data | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Email, name, password (stored hashed with bcrypt), sign-up date | The user |
| Security | IP address and email of failed login attempts (15 minutes), technical request log | The server |
| Matches | Team names, jersey colors, date and place of the match | The user |
| Video | Image of the people who appear (face, body, jersey number, clothing), **without audio**, at 10 frames per second and at most 720p | The user, from the app |
| Analytics | Positions, trajectories, metrics and insights per player, identified by an ID (for example, "J3") and jersey number | Generated by PP-Coach-Tracker |
| Tags | Jersey number, position and, if the user wants, a name, nickname or initials | The user |
| Consent | Each acceptance and withdrawal: version of the text, date, IP and app version | The app and the server |

**We do not process biometric data**: we do not do facial recognition or identify people by their features. We do not ask for health data, GPS location or contacts either. The image of minors is processed only if the user declared they have the consent of their parents or guardians.

## 4. What we use it for

**Primary purposes** (needed for the service):

1. Creating and protecting your account, and logging in.
2. Receiving the videos and generating the analytics: detecting and tracking players and the ball, tactical metrics and insights with artificial intelligence.
3. Showing you the results and letting you tag players.
4. Recording your consent and handling requests to exercise your ARCO rights.
5. Detecting abuse, unauthorized access attempts and technical failures.

**Secondary purposes:** none. We do not use the data for advertising, commercial profiling or to train artificial intelligence models. If we ever wanted to, we would ask for a separate consent, which you could refuse without losing the service.

## 7. Your rights (ARCO) and how to exercise them

You can **access** your data, **rectify** it, **cancel** (delete) it or **object** to its processing, as well as **revoke your consent** or limit its use.

- **Users:** from the app (Settings → withdraw the consent, and delete clips or matches) or by email to `[PRIVACY EMAIL]`.
- **People who appear in a video:** first contact the coach or club that recorded you. You can also write to us at `[PRIVACY EMAIL]` with the team, the date of the match and, if you can, a photo or description that lets us find the video; we will let the user know and delete the video and its analytics where appropriate.
- **Parents or guardians** can exercise these rights for the minors in their care, proving their representation.

The request must include your name, a way to contact you, the right you are exercising and the data it refers to. We answer within **20 business days** at most and, where appropriate, carry it out within the following 15 business days.

If you believe we did not handle your request, you can go to the data protection authority (Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno).

## 8. Security

- Encrypted connection (HTTPS) between the app and the server.
- Passwords hashed with bcrypt; sessions with signed tokens that expire after 30 days and are stored encrypted on the phone.
- Videos and configuration are stored outside the server's public folder; each user can only see their own clips.
- A limit on login attempts.
- Players' names are not sent to AI providers or to error logs.

## 9. Cookies

The app does not use cookies or advertising trackers. The page of this notice does not use analytics cookies.

We will publish any change at `https://ppcoachtracker.pepemxl.com/legal/en/privacy.html` with its new version and date. If the change affects the purposes or the recipients, the app will ask you to accept it before sending a new video.
